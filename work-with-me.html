Before you ask

The questions I'd ask too

Have you worked inside a club?

No. Which is why I publish the method instead of asserting the experience.

The recruitment work is entirely my own: six seasons of publicly available event data turned into corridor models, role clustering, custom metrics, complete mock commissions — and a public audit of my own transfer calls a season on, misses included. All of it at The Cutback, with the method open.

What my client work gives me is production discipline: visual and editorial output delivered on deadline, at scale, for audiences that notice mistakes. The two streams don't share data. They share standards.

You can judge the quality of both before paying for a minute of it. Very few people you could hire will offer you that.

Whose data do we use?

Yours. Clubs and agencies already licence a feed that covers the players they'd realistically sign, and I work inside it. Published work on The Cutback runs on publicly available data — which means a paid project has deeper coverage than anything you can read for free.

How fast can you start?

I work as a freelance analyst with existing commitments, so I take a limited number of projects at a time and quote timelines I can actually hold. If the window is tight, say so on the call and I'll tell you honestly whether it fits.

What if the data can't answer the question?

Then I say so on the scoping call, before you've spent anything. Event data is very good at some questions and poor at others — defenders and goalkeepers remain genuinely hard, and I'd rather tell you that up front than deliver a confident answer built on thin evidence.

Do you work in English?

Yes. Italian native, English professional, written and spoken.

Do you take retainers?

After a first project, yes — around €1,800 a month for ongoing shortlist and analysis support through a window. Better for both of us to start with something scoped.
