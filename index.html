What a Second opinion looks like

One player, six questions, one verdict. This is what you get, in the order you get it.

  1. The role he actually plays

    Position labels lie. I group players by where and how they act on the ball, so he's compared with players who do his job, not with everyone who wears his number.

  2. How good he is in that role

    His profile against every regular in his role and league, from chances and shooting to link play, carrying and defending. Then my finishing and passing models, which separate skill from luck, and how he's trended over his recent seasons. Where he stands out, and where he doesn't.

  3. How his move has gone for others

    Every league move has a track record. For strikers leaving the Championship, 47% of 241 held their level in their first season and 33% never won a regular place. That's the base rate: a prior, not a verdict on the player.

  4. What the data can't see

    Event data can't see runs in behind or how hard a striker presses. The report says plainly where the numbers stop, so you know what they leave open.

  5. What he should cost

    A fair price with a range, learned from what clubs actually paid for players like him. Price any player yourself in the price app.

  6. Sign, monitor or pass

    One word up front, a one-page answer behind it, then the detail if you want it, with the limits stated.
